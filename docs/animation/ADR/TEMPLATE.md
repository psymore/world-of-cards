## Context

What situation forced this decision? What constraints, evidence, or prior attempts led here? Cite concrete evidence from this repository (a bug, a benchmark, a real finding) the same way the Constitution's own principles do — not general best-practice reasoning in the abstract.

## Decision

State the decision in one or two sentences. Be concrete enough that a future reader can tell, unambiguously, whether it is still being followed.

## Alternatives Considered

What else was on the table, and why was it not chosen? Include options that were seriously evaluated, not a token list added for form.

## Consequences

What does this decision cost, and what does it buy? Include what becomes harder, not only what it enables.

## Revisit Trigger

What concrete, checkable condition should cause this decision to be reconsidered? (Constitution §7's own entries already use this pattern — e.g. "if a future principle can't be satisfied without X.") A decision recorded without a revisit trigger tends to quietly outlive its own justification.
